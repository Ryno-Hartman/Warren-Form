# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Adults exploring help with personal financial planning, investments, savings, retirement, estate planning, wills, trusts, or general financial guidance. They may be uncertain about the right service and want a low-pressure first conversation before sharing detailed financial information.

## Product Purpose

Provide a focused, trustworthy way for prospective clients to request a financial or estate-planning consultation. Success means Warren receives enough context to prepare for a useful first conversation without asking visitors for sensitive account, identity, or legal-document information.

## Positioning

A clear first step for people who want to make more considered personal financial decisions.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Request a consultation.
- Secondary CTA: None until Warren's direct contact details are confirmed.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Start with a conversation.
- Belief ladder: this is relevant to my personal goals; I can explain my situation in plain language; I will not be pressured or asked for sensitive credentials; the next step is a conversation, not a commitment.
- Proof on hand: none yet. Do not invent credentials, testimonials, client counts, performance claims, or partner logos.

## Brand Personality

Composed, direct, and reassuring. The voice is plain-spoken and respectful, with confidence coming from clarity rather than claims or financial jargon.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a corporate bank brochure, trading platform, business-finance application, or aggressive investment funnel. Avoid luxury wealth clichés, fake market charts, guaranteed-return language, and imagery that implies credentials or offices Warren has not confirmed.

## Design Principles

1. Ask only for information that helps prepare the first conversation.
2. Make privacy boundaries visible before visitors share personal context.
3. Design for a phone held in one hand before adapting to larger screens.
4. Use confident hierarchy and restrained language instead of financial-industry spectacle.
5. Keep all identity, credentials, and claims generic until Warren confirms the details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with strong text contrast, visible keyboard focus, 44px minimum touch targets, semantic labels and fieldsets, clear inline errors, reduced-motion support, and plain-language instructions.
